# Grounded personal thoughts

Compose personal thoughts from the current requirements, actual implementation, real results and relevant principles. The report has one student author looking back on the assignment. Use natural first person for supported choices, implementation, observations and judgments throughout the relevant discussion, alongside objective explanations of concepts, formulas and routine processes. Do not impose pronoun counts, proportions or chapter quotas, mechanically replace subjects, or invent a rationale for every setting. Existing genuine human judgments remain relevant sources. Missing replies or acceptance records do not prevent writing, continuing or checking a complete report.

Develop the specific understanding and tradeoffs that this completed work supports, with detail determined by the current argument. Class differences and error analysis can explain the results without inventing business, deployment or error-cost requirements to advise a hypothetical model user. A concrete choice or useful insight may enter the relevant discussion; no fixed count or standalone reflection chapter is required. Follow the language-first [shared standard](report-quality.md), including deletion-first editing and the default omission of limitations discussion.

Choose future discussion from the assignment requirements, current request and its actual purpose. When neither a requirement nor continuing work calls for it, finish with the understanding of the completed assignment rather than adding a future-work section, invented commitments or a generic experiment checklist under another title. For a one-off assignment, finish with the concrete understanding gained from doing it; an optional improvement need not be added merely to make the reflection more complete. Other assignments that require an outlook or have real continuing tasks should address them as needed, distinguishing possible directions from activities actually scheduled. A request to discuss improvements does not itself establish a commitment to implement them.

Distinguish observation, code reasoning and future proposals. Actual Agent implementation, debugging and runs are real assignment process and may be described accurately. Invented struggles, feelings, reading or experiments are not evidence. Ask only about a substantive ambiguity that affects the current task, while completing independent work.

## Current paragraphs and changed evidence

Readable notes identify the paragraphs actually used and their basis. Keep original recommendations, verbatim replies, attribution, decisions and history readable when present. Recommended, mixed and not-linked are historical provenance descriptions, not readiness verdicts; neither invent acceptance nor remove links to obtain completion. In read-only/advice scope, inspect and respond in chat without writing records.

Compare relevant source changes with each selected claim. If the new basis still supports it, retain the paragraph and record the actual comparison when writing is authorized. If its meaning must change, revise the affected content, retain earlier judgments as history, and refresh the affected language, independent review and build evidence. Evidence damage remains a real gap. Unused questions and unrelated changes do not invalidate supported content. A whole-file hash alone cannot settle semantic impact.

Completion follows current requirements, grounded content and actual report evidence, including the complete language method and independent review. No routine interview or human acceptance stage follows. See [stable report project](report.md).
